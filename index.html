Owned equipment · Elk Grove, California

Inflatable carnival games

Eight ground-level game booths on shared blowers. Classic midway games — ring toss, darts, sports tosses — not bounce houses, not water slides, not climb/slide attractions.

Public rental rates are not posted. This page identifies the owned equipment for insurance and partner review. Inquiries: contact@cpalss.com.

The eight stations

Magic Jump carnival-game series (2025). Units link on rear inflation tubes; up to three stations share one 1 hp blower. We own four Kodiak blowers with the pack.
